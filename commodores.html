Smash

Taught

2x2 shotgun, center sits at 6 yards. Outside kid takes three steps and stops. Inside kid on that side runs to the far corner. Thrower looks at the kid who stopped first, corner if the defender jumps the sit. Flip both ways. First throw must land past the line. 5-second clock.

“2x2” means two receivers on each side. “Shotgun” means the thrower starts a few steps behind the center.

Overload 50/51

Taught

Extra kids to one side, throw the first open one past the line. 50 one way, 51 the flip.

“Overload” just means more receivers line up on one side than the other.

This is the game defense. Six named spots. One helper with no spot. Do not rush the thrower. Hip-stay / pick-a-guy / Man-Free is a drill only — never drawn as the game look.

Stay on a hip

Drill only

Pick a kid. Stay on their hip. This is a practice drill so kids learn to stay close. It is not the game defense. Do not call it in a game. Do not treat it as a second look.

Coach terms for the same drill: hip-stay / pick-a-guy / Man-Free. Never the plan.

Two defense groups

Set before the quarter

We use two defense groups. No free swapping in the middle of a quarter. Set who is in each group before the quarter starts, then keep that group on the field.

About 13 kids: two groups of about seven. Adjust the last spot so both groups have a helper.
Do not add:
  • No rush. The league does not allow rushing the thrower.
  • No second package. One game look all year unless staff later writes a new one here.
  • If they look lost, make the spots bigger. Do not sneak hip-stay / man back in.
  • Do not add “zone” as a new package. The six areas already are that look.
