Six spots + one helper

Game defense

Six kids each cover a spot (an area): left short, middle short, right short, left deep, middle deep, right deep. Keep eyes on the thrower and protect your area instead of following one receiver everywhere. When a receiver leaves your area, let the next kid take them. The seventh kid roams in the middle and helps where the ball goes. No rush on the QB. Finish with a one-hand touch below the neck.

This is the only game defense through Aug 30. Same look in live reps.
