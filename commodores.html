Game defense · six areas + one helper

Base from week 1

Six kids each cover a spot (an area). Keep eyes on the thrower and protect your area instead of following one receiver everywhere. When a receiver leaves your area, let the next kid take them. The seventh kid roams in the middle and helps where the ball goes. No rush on the QB. Finish with a one-hand touch below the neck.

Hip-stay / “pick a guy” / Man-Free is a drill only. It is not the game defense or another package.

Smash

Taught

2x2 shotgun, center sits at 6 yards. Outside kid takes three steps and stops. Inside kid on that side runs to the far corner. Thrower looks at the kid who stopped first, corner if the defender jumps the sit. Flip both ways. First throw must land past the line. 5-second clock.

“2x2” means two receivers on each side. “Shotgun” means the thrower starts a few steps behind the center.

Overload 50/51

Taught

Extra kids to one side, throw the first open one past the line. 50 one way, 51 the flip.

“Overload” just means more receivers line up on one side than the other.
