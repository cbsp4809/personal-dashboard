What this app is

This is the staff field book for FWE JV Vanderbilt Commodores, Fall 2026. Peter, Chris, and Ben use it. It is not a parent app and it is not a kid app.

Who uses what Coaches use this book. Parents stay on GameChanger. Kids only get a secret watch link when staff publishes plays — they never sign in here.
  • Staff — schedule, playbook, lineups, practice notes, ratings, and the play builder.
  • Parents — GameChanger for team comms, games, and family updates. Do not send them this login page.
  • Kids — a read-only watch page with a secret watch.html?t= link. No coach email. No roster. No editor.

The book lives on the Cloudflare Worker, not the public GitHub Pages site. Cloudflare Access is off. Sign-in is the coach email and password only.

Install / reinstall on iPhone or iPad

This is a Safari web app, not an App Store download. Chrome and other browsers cannot add it the same way.

If an old icon is already on the Home Screen Delete that icon first. Long-press it → Remove App → Delete Bookmark. Then add it again from the Worker URL. An old github.io icon will not open the live book.
  1. Open commodores.html in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with the up arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Name it Commodores (short name Dores) and tap Add.

The gold-star icon should open the sign-in screen full-screen. To reinstall later, delete the icon and repeat the same four steps.

Sign in

Use the coach email Chris invited, plus the password you set. That is Supabase Auth. There is no Cloudflare Access screen and no PIN.

  • The phone or iPad stays signed in until you tap Lock.
  • The same login works on the field book and on the play animator.
  • Only allowlisted coaches see roster, ratings, notes, and plays.
  • First names only after you are in.

Forgot the password or your email is refused? Text Chris. Do not share passwords in this manual, in texts to parents, or on GameChanger.

The five tabs

Primary pages across the top: Team / Today, Playbook, Plays, Lineup, Practice. Season board is a reference link under the tabs — schedule, milestones, goals. It is not a sixth working page.

Team / Today

Home screen after sign-in. Next practice, the official SBMSA slate, the two-play / one-defense reminder, and the shared roster with staff ratings. Score as yourself. Order stays frozen while you rate.

Playbook

The staff book kids get taught from. Offense and Defense sub-tabs. Offense lists the saved drawings. Defense is still six areas + one helper, no rush. Hip-stay / pick-a-guy is a drill only — never the game look.

Lineup

Two quarter units: Q1/Q3 and Q2/Q4. Each has 7 offense and 7 defense. Mark Present / Out first. The play-time rule check uses only the kids who showed (2+2 or one-way; short-roster both-way math if we are short). No mid-quarter swapping.

Offense letters: Q · C · X · A · B · Y · Z. Those names are what the play builder uses.

Practice

Thursday 5:00 p.m. and Sunday 1:00 p.m. at Frostwood through Dec 6. Date list, drill picker, and the last-practice → next-practice notes staff share. Write Thursday’s plan before you leave Sunday.

Plays

Coach-only animator and draw editor. Build the real routes here, save them, then teach from Playbook. Same page also lives at the Plays URL if you want it on its own Home Screen icon.

Play builder and kids links

Open Plays in the field book, or go straight to the animator. Draw the seven spots. Labels come from the current lineup in this fixed order:

QCXABYZ

Q is the thrower (white). C is the center (gold). X left out (sky), Z right out (salmon), A / B / Y inside (orange / mint / lilac). That same color is the name bubble, route, and arrow. Coach Plays uses the selected Q1/Q3 or Q2/Q4 game lineup. The all-season kids link uses roster practice tags instead: after a kid chooses Watching as, only that first name labels their tagged route bubbles while other routes fade. Optional jersey numbers appear only when staff has entered them. The route letter stays visible on the path.

  • Draw — drag a letter to place it. Tap a letter, then finger-draw its route. Sit = no route.
  • Save — give it a number and a short title. That is the staff drawing, not a guessed card.
  • Play — Play / Pause, scrub, Flip, Reset, or full-screen on the iPad (rotate either way). Full screen keeps the play name visible.
Publishing to kids is a different door Kids never use the coach login. Mark Publish this play, then Republish names + plays. Copy the one secret practice link. It looks like watch.html?t= plus a long token. Kids pick their first name under Watching as; their tagged route letters and D1–D7 zones light up while the rest fade. No lineup, editor, or save controls appear.
  • Send kids only that watch link — not commodores.html, not plays.html, not this manual.
  • After you change routes, practice tags, or jersey numbers, tap Republish names + plays or the watch page stays stale.
  • Rotate secret link kills the old URL. Make a new one only if a link leaked.

Chris traces plays into the playbook

Chris will trace the real routes into the playbook so 23/26, 38, and the rest are the lines we actually teach — not guessed cards. If a drawing looks wrong, say so.

Ask for feedback If a route, a name on a letter, or a kids-link snapshot is off, tell Chris. The book stays living. Better a one-line correction than a kid learning the wrong stem.

Troubleshooting

The Home Screen icon opens the wrong page or an old book

Delete the icon and add it again from Safari on the Worker URL. Old github.io icons are dead.

I do not see Add to Home Screen

You are probably in Chrome or a Facebook/Messages in-app browser. Open the link in Safari, then Share.

Sign-in says the account is not on the staff list

The email is not on the coach allowlist, or you typed a different address than the invite. Check with Chris. Do not create a new account on your own.

I am signed in but roster or plays are empty

Check the network, pull to refresh, or Lock and sign in again. If it is still empty, Chris needs to look at the shared staff row — do not rebuild the roster in the page.

Kids say the watch link does nothing

The link must include ?t= and the full token. A rotated link is dead. After roster-tag or route edits, republish. Kids should not be sent the coach pages.

Play names do not match who is in that spot

For coach Plays, fix the Q1/Q3 or Q2/Q4 lineup. For the kids practice link, fix that player’s Q/C/X/A/B/Y/Z roster tags. Then republish; the app never invents names or jersey numbers.

I got a Cloudflare email-code screen

That should not appear. Access is off. You want the gold field-book sign-in card only. If a code screen shows up, stop and tell Chris — do not put Access back.
