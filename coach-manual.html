What this app is

This is the staff field book for FWE JV Vanderbilt Commodores, Fall 2026. Peter, Chris, and Ben use it. It is not a parent app and it is not a kid app.

Who uses what Coaches use this book. Parents stay on GameChanger. Kids only get a secret watch link when staff publishes plays — they never sign in here.
  • Staff — Team / Today, playbook, lineups, practice notes, ratings, and the play builder.
  • Parents — GameChanger for team comms, games, and family updates. Do not send them this login page.
  • Kids — a read-only watch page with a secret watch.html?t= link. No coach email. No roster editor. No save.

The book lives on the Cloudflare Worker, not the public GitHub Pages site. Cloudflare Access is off. Sign-in is the coach email and password only.

Install / reinstall on iPhone or iPad

This is a Safari web app, not an App Store download. Chrome and other browsers cannot add it the same way.

If an old icon is already on the Home Screen Delete that icon first. Long-press it → Remove App → Delete Bookmark. Then add it again from the Worker URL. An old github.io icon will not open the live book.
  1. Open commodores.html in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with the up arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Name it Commodores (short name Dores) and tap Add.

The gold-star icon should open the sign-in screen full-screen. To reinstall later, delete the icon and repeat the same four steps.

Sign in and reset password

Use the coach email Chris invited, plus the password you set. That is Supabase Auth. There is no Cloudflare Access screen and no PIN.

  • The phone or iPad stays signed in until you tap Lock.
  • The same login works on the field book and on the play animator.
  • Only allowlisted coaches see roster, ratings, notes, and plays.
  • First names only after you are in.

Forgot password

Tap Forgot password? on the field-book login or on Plays. Enter your coach email. The reset email opens the Worker page reset.html — not the Supabase dashboard.

The five tabs

Primary pages across the top: Team / Today, Playbook, Plays, Lineup, Practice. Season board is a reference link under the tabs — schedule, milestones, goals. It is not a sixth working page.

Team / Today

Home screen after sign-in. Next practice, the official SBMSA slate, the two-play / one-defense reminder, and the shared roster with staff ratings. Score as yourself. Order stays frozen while you rate. Practice tags live on each kid’s roster card.

Playbook

The staff book kids get taught from. Offense and Defense sub-tabs. Offense lists the saved drawings. Defense is still six areas + one helper, no rush. Hip-stay / pick-a-guy is a drill only — never the game look.

Plays

Coach-only animator and draw editor. Build the real routes here, save them, then teach from Playbook. Same page also lives at the Plays URL if you want it on its own Home Screen icon.

Lineup

Two quarter units: Q1/Q3 and Q2/Q4. Each has 7 offense and 7 defense. Mark Present / Out first. The play-time rule check uses only the kids who showed (2+2 or one-way; short-roster both-way math if we are short). No mid-quarter swapping.

Say Q1/Q3 and Q2/Q4 to parents Those are the names on the boards. Never call the units A/B in front of parents or kids.

Practice

Thursday 5:00 p.m. and Sunday 1:00 p.m. at Frostwood through Dec 6. Date list, drill picker, and the last-practice → next-practice notes staff share. Write Thursday’s plan before you leave Sunday.

Practice tags and the gold 1

On Team / Today, open a kid’s roster card. Offense chips are Q C X A B Y Z. Defense chips are D1–D7.

  • First tap on an empty side adds that tag. It becomes the primary if that side had none.
  • One gold 1 per side — one primary offense letter, one primary defense zone.
  • Tap a tagged backup chip to promote it to the gold 1.
  • Tap the current gold 1 to clear that primary (the rest stay in order).
  • Array order is rank. Index 0 is the primary. Never sort tags.

Kids learn more than one spot over the year. The gold 1 is “open this first.” Extra tags stay on the card so the watch page can offer a toggle.

Practice tags vs game-day lineup

These are two different name sources. Do not mix them up.

  • Practice / kids watch uses roster practice tags (and optional jerseys). After a kid picks Watching as, the gold-1 letter or zone lights first.
  • Game animator can use the Q1/Q3 or Q2/Q4 lineup. Coach Plays has a Q1/Q3 names / Q2/Q4 names switch for that.

Publishing captures the practice tags, not the quarter lineup. If a kids name looks wrong, fix the roster tags, then Push to kids. If a coach Play name looks wrong, fix the Q1/Q3 or Q2/Q4 board.

Play builder and kids link

Open Plays in the field book, or go straight to the animator. Draw the seven spots. Coach labels come from the selected Q1/Q3 or Q2/Q4 lineup in this fixed order:

QCXABYZ

Q is the thrower (white). C is the center (gold). X left out (sky), Z right out (salmon), A / B / Y inside (orange / mint / lilac).

Draw is scroll-safe

  • A straight swipe on empty turf scrolls the page. It does not start a route.
  • Drag a letter to place it.
  • Tap a letter chip to arm it, then draw from that letter. Sit = no route.
  • The field only captures a draw after a letter is armed — not for the whole Draw session.

Sticky bottom bar

Publish, Push, and Save live in the sticky bar at the bottom of the phone. You do not have to scroll to the end of the page to publish.

  • Publish this play / Remove from kids — mark the selected play for the kids snapshot.
  • Push to kids — appears when any published play is stale (routes, tags, or jerseys changed).
  • Save play — shows in Draw.

Play chips and filters

Each saved play chip shows Kids, Coach only, or Edited (saved since the last kids snapshot). Filters above the lists: All / Kids / Not on kids.

Kids link strip — at the top

Above Play / Draw: last-pushed time (Chicago, like Pushed Sat 4:01 p.m., or Not on kids yet) and Copy.

  • Copy the one secret practice link. It looks like watch.html?t= plus a long token.
  • Change link is tucked behind a tap. Rotate secret link asks for confirm, then kills the old URL. Use that only if a link leaked.
Kids never use the coach login Send kids only that watch link — not commodores.html, not plays.html, not this manual. After you change routes, practice tags, or jersey numbers, tap Push to kids or the watch page stays stale.

What kids see

The watch home is: Watching as, then Offense plays, then Defense with one Base zone card. Goal line is later — it is not shown.

  • Pick a first name under Watching as. That choice drives both sides.
  • The primary letter or zone loads first. Extra tags toggle at the top of each play.
  • Kids learn multiple positions over the year. Some have a main spot; extras stay available.
  • Base zone is static: six areas + D7 deep behind D5. Nothing on defense moves. No rush.
  • Offense plays have an optional Defense overlay of those same static zones.
  • 0.5× is the default play speed so they can see the routes.

Parents have their own short guide on the watch page Help link. Do not send them this coach manual.

Troubleshooting

The Home Screen icon opens the wrong page or an old book

Delete the icon and add it again from Safari on the Worker URL. Old github.io icons are dead.

I do not see Add to Home Screen

You are probably in Chrome or a Facebook/Messages in-app browser. Open the link in Safari, then Share.

Forgot password sent me somewhere that is not reset.html

Stop. The reset must open the Worker reset.html page. Do not use the Supabase dashboard. Request a new link from Forgot password, or text Chris.

Sign-in says the account is not on the staff list

The email is not on the coach allowlist, or you typed a different address than the invite. Check with Chris. Do not create a new account on your own.

I am signed in but roster or plays are empty

Check the network, pull to refresh, or Lock and sign in again. If it is still empty, Chris needs to look at the shared staff row — do not rebuild the roster in the page.

Kids say the watch link does nothing

The link must include ?t= and the full token. A rotated link is dead. After roster-tag or route edits, Push to kids. Kids should not be sent the coach pages.

Play names do not match who is in that spot

For coach Plays, fix the Q1/Q3 or Q2/Q4 lineup. For the kids practice link, fix that player’s Q/C/X/A/B/Y/Z or D1–D7 tags and the gold 1. Then Push to kids. The app never invents names or jersey numbers.

I cannot find Publish

It sits in the sticky bar at the bottom of Plays: Publish / Push / Save. The Kids link Copy is at the top.

I got a Cloudflare email-code screen

That should not appear. Access is off. You want the gold field-book sign-in card only. If a code screen shows up, stop and tell Chris — do not put Access back.
