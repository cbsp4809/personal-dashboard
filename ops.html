LinkedIn cheat sheet

Drafts. ChatGPT drops finished posts in Drive ready/ as YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md. Sydney files them on Content.

Statuses

  1. Ready — new draft for Chris
  2. Needs you — his call (names, claims, Iris/PopCam, etc.)
  3. Needs changes — he wants edits; leave a note; ChatGPT revises; back to Ready
  4. Approved — locked; Sydney attaches photo; Studio Pod PR posts
  5. Scheduled — waiting Tue 11 / Wed 3 / Thu 1 CT (or a time he sets)
  6. Posted — live; Drive file moves to posted/

Chris. Open the card → read the ChatGPT copy (do not rewrite unless he asks) → Approve, Needs changes (+ note), or Needs you if a flag looks off → confirm personal LinkedIn (default) and the photo.

Sydney / PR. After Approved → photo on the card → PR posts → live link → Posted.

Cadence. Tue 11:00 a.m., Wed 3:00 p.m., Thu 1:00 p.m. America/Chicago. Destination default: personal LinkedIn.
