Lineup

Q1/Q3 unit · Q2/Q4 unit · 7 O + 7 D each

SBMSA Fall 7on7 · how the card has to work
  • Mark who is Present / Out for this practice or game first. Quarter units only use the kids who showed.
  • No free substitution. Injury only. Two units of 7 are set before each quarter — no one-for-one mid-quarter swap.
  • Q1/Q3 plays the first and third quarters. Q2/Q4 plays the second and fourth quarters.
  • Every present kid starts two full quarters on O and two on D, or plays all four quarters on one side only.
  • 14 spots a quarter. With fewer than 12 present you need more both-ways (11→3, 10→4, … 7→all both-ways). Need at least 7 to play.
  • O letters: Q · C · X · A · B · Y · Z. Facing the ball, D1/D2/D3 are front defense-right to defense-left; D4 is behind D3, D5 behind D2, D6 behind D1, and D7 is the deep safety.
  • Fastest: tap a spot to pick from the kids who studied it — their tags show right there — or hit Fill from preferred to auto-set a whole unit from what each kid was told, then tweak.
  • Still works: tap a name then a spot, or drag on a computer. Tap Clear, then a placed name, to empty a spot.
