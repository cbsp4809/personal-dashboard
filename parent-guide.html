How to use Commodores Plays

A short guide for parents and kids. Close this page when you are done and go back to your play link.

Last updated: Aug 29, 2026

Open it

Open the secret play link on a phone, tablet, or computer. Bookmark it so you do not have to hunt for the message later.

Watching as

At the top, tap Watching as and pick your kid’s first name. That choice stays while you look at offense and defense.

More than one spot

Kids learn several positions this year Some kids have a main spot. The app opens that primary letter or zone first. If your kid has extra spots, toggle them at the top of each play.

Offense

  • Tap a play, then tap Play to watch the routes.
  • Use 0.5× when you are learning. It is slower on purpose.
  • You can also try 1× or 2×, or use full screen on a tablet.

Defense

Tap Base zone. You will see six areas plus a deep helper. Nothing on defense moves. Your kid’s main spot is lit. Extra zones can be toggled at the top.

On an offense play you can turn a Defense overlay on if you want to see those same areas under the routes.

Keep the link in the team

Do not share the secret link outside the team This page is only for our kids and families. It is not the coach login. Coaches have a different app. Parents still use GameChanger for team messages and games.
